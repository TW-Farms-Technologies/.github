### 🚜 For players

Our FS25 maps and mods are **always free**, and you download them from [twfarms.uk](https://twfarms.uk), not from here.

- **Dover Mills 16x** (Iowa): big fields, wide roads, and room for the big kit
- **TW Farm Productions**: production chains that work on any map
- **TW Dairy**: a dairy with its own milk trigger and rotary parlour
- **TW British Asset Pack**: hedges, walls, signs and road markings for UK maps

Found a bug? Tell us in [Discord](https://discord.gg/FsuBuQB3Zy); that's where we pick them up fastest.

### 🛠️ What we build

- **Farm Forge**: our map-making tool for terrain, roads and layouts that actually work for truck and trailer
- **Mini Forge** (alpha): turns 2D references into 3D models ready for GIANTS Editor
- **AI Workshop**: research into AI help for map and mod building

We're a member of NVIDIA Inception. Our code is private for now, so this page is mainly here so you can find us.
